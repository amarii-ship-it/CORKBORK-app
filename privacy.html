CORKBORK privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026.

The short version

CORKBORK has no server and no accounts. Your boards, notes and files are saved in your own browser and, if you connect it, in your own Google Drive. Nobody else, including the people who make CORKBORK, receives or can see them.

What is stored, and where

  • On your device: everything you create, in your browser's own storage, and optionally in a folder you choose on your computer.
  • In your Google Drive, only if you press Connect Google Drive: a folder named CORKBORK holding your boards, pictures and original files. Boards you mark private are never uploaded.

Google access

CORKBORK asks Google for one permission, drive.file. It lets CORKBORK see and change only the files CORKBORK itself created in your Drive. It cannot see any other file in your Drive. CORKBORK also reads your Google account's email address, only to make the sign-in window close by itself next time; it is stored on your device and nowhere else.

CORKBORK's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to save and sync your own work for you, is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone.

Other connections

When you paste a link, CORKBORK may ask a public preview service for that page's title and picture. Some tools (Word layout view, text recognition) download open-source helper code the first time you use them. None of these receive your boards or files.

Removing your data

Disconnect in Settings → Cloud sync, then delete the CORKBORK folder in Google Drive. To remove CORKBORK's access entirely, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes the copy on that device.
